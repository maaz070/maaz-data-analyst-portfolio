# 👥 Customer Demographics & Segmentation Dashboard

**Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel  
**Dataset:** Synthetic customer data (demographics, income, and segments)  

---

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze and visualize customer demographics, income distribution, and tenure patterns across different regions and segments, providing insights into customer behavior and business opportunities.

---

## 🧮 Steps Performed
1. Cleaned and formatted raw customer data.  
2. Created calculated fields for **Income Bracket**, **Tenure Group**, and **Customer Segment**.  
3. Built pivot tables for:
   - Gender-wise customer distribution  
   - Income by region and occupation  
   - Tenure group breakdown  
   - Customer segments by generation  
4. Designed an interactive **Excel dashboard** using slicers and charts.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Millennials** and **Gen X** represent the majority of active customers.  
- **Retail** and **Corporate** are the dominant customer segments.  
- **High-income customers** are concentrated in the **North and East** regions.  
- **Tenure** strongly correlates with customer loyalty, especially for Corporate clients.
